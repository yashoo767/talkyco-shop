About TalkyCo

We make it simple and safe to access the over-the-counter healthcare products you need, delivered right to your door.

Who We Are

TalkyCo LLC is a U.S.-based online retailer specializing in over-the-counter (OTC) healthcare products. We are committed to making quality, reliable OTC products accessible through a safe, transparent, and easy-to-use online storefront.

Our shop — operating at shop.talkyco.com — offers a carefully curated catalog of OTC-eligible products across categories including pain relief, allergy care, digestive health, first aid, personal care, and vitamins & wellness.

All products sold through the TalkyCo Shop are over-the-counter eligible. We do not sell prescription medications.

What We Sell

TalkyCo Shop exclusively sells over-the-counter healthcare products. Our catalog includes:

  • Pain relievers and fever reducers (NSAIDs, acetaminophen)
  • Antihistamines and allergy/cold care products
  • Digestive health aids (antacids, PPIs)
  • First aid supplies (bandages, antiseptic ointments)
  • Vitamins and dietary supplements
  • Personal care and skin care products

What We Do Not Sell

The following are not available through TalkyCo Shop:

  • Prescription medications of any kind
  • Controlled substances
  • Cannabis or CBD products
  • Vape or e-cigarette products
  • Kratom or other prohibited substances
  • Drug paraphernalia
