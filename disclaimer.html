Read Product Labels

Customers should read all product labels, warnings, and directions before use. Product label information supersedes any information on this website. Always follow the labeled directions and warnings for any product you purchase.

Labels contain important information including:

  • Active and inactive ingredients

TalkyCo recommends that customers consult an appropriate healthcare professional when necessary, including but not limited to situations where:
