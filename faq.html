Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about our products, ordering, shipping, returns, and SMS program.

TalkyCo sells over-the-counter (OTC) healthcare products only. Our catalog includes pain relievers, allergy and cold care, digestive health aids, first aid supplies, vitamins and dietary supplements, and personal care products. We do not sell prescription medications, controlled substances, cannabis or CBD products, vape products, or any other prohibited items.

No. All products sold by TalkyCo are over-the-counter eligible, meaning they do not require a prescription. However, some OTC products have age restrictions or specific usage conditions — please read all label directions before use. If you have questions about whether a product is appropriate for you, consult a healthcare professional.

Every product page on TalkyCo Shop includes complete product information: active ingredients, inactive ingredients (where applicable), directions for use, warnings, manufacturer information, and quantity/size details. Click "View Product" on any product listing to see the full information before purchasing.

All products we carry are standard over-the-counter healthcare products from established manufacturers. As with all OTC products, please read labels carefully, follow directions, and consult a healthcare professional if you have any medical conditions, take prescription medications, or have questions about whether a product is appropriate for you. See our Product Disclaimer for more information.

Browse our product catalog, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You'll provide your shipping address and payment information, and review your order before completing the purchase. You do not need an account to check out — guest checkout is available.

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) and debit cards. Payment is processed securely at checkout using SSL encryption.

Orders can be cancelled or modified within 1 hour of placement, before they are processed for shipment. To request a change, contact us immediately at support@talkyco.com or call 1-800-TALKYCO. Once an order has been shipped, it can no longer be modified, but you may be eligible for a return per our return policy.

Standard shipping is free on orders of $35 or more. For orders under $35, standard shipping is $5.99. Expedited shipping (2–3 business days) is available for an additional fee at checkout. See our Shipping Policy for full details.

Standard shipping typically takes 5–7 business days. Expedited shipping takes 2–3 business days. Orders placed before 2 PM ET on business days are generally processed the same day.

At this time, TalkyCo Shop ships to eligible U.S. addresses only. We do not currently ship internationally or to P.O. boxes.

Unopened, undamaged products may be returned within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. Opened OTC healthcare products generally cannot be returned for safety reasons. For damaged or defective items, please contact us within 7 days of receipt. See our full Returns & Refund Policy.

Contact us at support@talkyco.com or call 1-800-TALKYCO with your order number to initiate a return. We'll provide a prepaid return label for eligible returns.
