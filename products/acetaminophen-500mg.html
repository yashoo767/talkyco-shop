About This Product

Acetaminophen 500mg Caplets provide extra-strength pain and fever relief that is gentle on the stomach. Unlike NSAIDs (such as ibuprofen or aspirin), acetaminophen does not irritate the stomach lining, making it a preferred choice for those with stomach sensitivities.

Relieves

  • Headache and sinus pain
  • Fever
  • Muscle aches and back pain
  • Minor arthritis pain
  • Toothache
  • Cold and flu symptoms

Directions

Do not take more than directed. Liver warning: Taking too much acetaminophen may cause liver damage.
  • Adults and children 12 years and over: Take 2 caplets every 6 hours while symptoms last.
  • Do not take more than 6 caplets in 24 hours unless directed by a doctor.
  • Children under 12: Ask a doctor.

Swallow caplets whole with water. May be taken with or without food.

Important Warnings

Liver warning: This product contains acetaminophen. Severe liver damage may occur if you take: more than 6 caplets in 24 hours, with other drugs containing acetaminophen, or 3 or more alcoholic drinks every day.
  • Allergy alert: Acetaminophen may cause severe skin reactions. Stop use and seek medical help if skin redness or blisters appear.
  • Do not use with any other drug containing acetaminophen (prescription or OTC).
  • Ask a doctor before use if you have liver disease or take the blood thinning drug warfarin.
  • Keep out of reach of children. In case of overdose, get medical help or contact Poison Control immediately.
