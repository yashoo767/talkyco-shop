About This Product

This variety pack includes an assortment of flexible fabric bandages designed to provide comfortable, secure coverage for everyday minor wounds. The flexible fabric construction moves with your body while maintaining a firm seal to keep out dirt and germs.

  • Flexible fabric that moves with your skin
  • Absorbent wound pad that stays clean
  • Non-stick pad to avoid sticking to wound
  • Sterile packaging preserves product integrity
  • Latex-free for sensitive skin
  • 100% skin-safe adhesive

How to Use

  1. Clean the wound gently with mild soap and water or a wound cleanser.
  2. If desired, apply a first aid ointment (such as bacitracin) to the wound.
  3. Open the bandage wrapper carefully without touching the wound pad.
  4. Place the wound pad over the injury, adhesive side down.
  5. Press firmly on the adhesive strips to secure.
  6. Change the bandage at least once daily or when wet or soiled.

Warnings

  • For external use only.
  • Do not use on deep puncture wounds, serious burns, or large wounds — seek medical attention for these.
  • Discontinue use if skin irritation, rash, or allergic reaction occurs.
  • If wound does not begin to heal within a few days, consult a healthcare professional.
  • Keep out of reach of children under 3 years — bandages are a choking hazard.
