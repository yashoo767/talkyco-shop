About This Product

Bacitracin Triple Antibiotic First Aid Ointment contains three active antibiotic ingredients that work together to help prevent bacterial infection in minor skin injuries. When applied to a wound and covered with a bandage, the ointment helps maintain a moist environment that promotes faster healing.

  • Helps prevent infection in minor cuts, scrapes, and burns
  • Three antibiotics for broad-spectrum protection
  • Promotes moist wound healing
  • Easy-to-apply tube format

IngredientAmountPurpose

Inactive Ingredients

Petrolatum (white), mineral oil.

Directions

  • Clean the affected area thoroughly.
  • Apply a small amount of ointment (an amount equal to the surface area of the tip of a finger) to the area 1 to 3 times daily.
  • Cover with a sterile bandage if desired.
  • Children under 2 years of age: Ask a doctor before use.

Warnings — For External Use Only

Allergy alert: Do not use if you are allergic to any of the active ingredients (bacitracin, neomycin, polymyxin B).
  • Do not use in the eyes, over large areas of the body, or if the person has deep or puncture wounds, animal bites, or serious burns.
  • Stop use and ask a doctor if the condition persists or gets worse, a rash or other allergic reaction develops, or if condition does not improve within 1 week.
  • Keep out of reach of children. In case of accidental ingestion, contact Poison Control.
