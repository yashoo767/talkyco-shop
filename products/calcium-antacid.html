About This Product

Calcium Antacid Chewable Tablets provide fast, on-contact relief from acid-related digestive discomfort. Calcium carbonate works by directly neutralizing excess stomach acid to relieve heartburn, acid indigestion, sour stomach, and upset stomach. Each serving also provides 500mg of calcium to support bone health.

  • Fast-acting — starts working in seconds
  • Chewable — no water needed
  • Assorted fruit flavors (cherry, orange, lemon)
  • Bonus calcium for bone support

Directions

  • Adults: Chew 2 to 4 tablets as symptoms occur, or as directed by a doctor.
  • Do not take more than 15 tablets in 24 hours.
  • Do not use the maximum dosage for more than 2 weeks.
  • Children under 12: Ask a doctor.

Warnings

  • Ask a doctor before use if you have kidney disease.
  • Stop use and ask a doctor if symptoms last more than 2 weeks.
  • Keep out of reach of children.
