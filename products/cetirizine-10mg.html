About This Product

Cetirizine HCl 10mg Tablets are a low-sedating second-generation antihistamine for 24-hour relief of allergy symptoms. Cetirizine selectively targets H1 histamine receptors to reduce allergy responses without the significant sedation associated with older antihistamines.

Relieves

  • Runny nose and sneezing
  • Itchy, watery eyes
  • Itchy nose or throat
  • Hives and skin itching
  • Indoor allergens: pet dander, dust mites, mold
  • Outdoor allergens: pollen, grass, trees

Inactive Ingredients

Carnauba wax, croscarmellose sodium, hypromellose, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, polyethylene glycol, titanium dioxide.

Directions

  • Adults and children 6 years and over: One 10mg tablet once daily; not more than one tablet in 24 hours.
  • Children 2 to under 6 years: Ask a doctor.
  • Adults 65 years and over: Ask a doctor.
  • Consumers with kidney disease: Ask a doctor.

Take with or without water. May be taken with or without food.

Warnings

  • Do not use if you have ever had an allergic reaction to cetirizine hydrochloride or hydroxyzine, or any of the inactive ingredients.
  • Ask a doctor before use if you have kidney or liver disease.
  • May cause drowsiness: Be careful when driving a motor vehicle or operating machinery. Avoid concurrent use of alcohol.
  • If pregnant or breastfeeding, ask a health professional before use.
  • Keep out of reach of children.
