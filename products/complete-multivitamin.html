Suggested Use

  • Adults 18 and over: Take 1 tablet daily with food.
  • Do not chew — swallow whole with a full glass of water.
  • Best taken with a meal to maximize absorption and minimize any stomach upset.
  • Do not exceed 1 tablet daily unless directed by a healthcare professional.

Warnings

  • Keep out of reach of children.
  • Do not use if seal is broken or missing.
  • Contains Iron — accidental overdose of iron-containing products is a leading cause of fatal poisoning in children under 6. Keep this product out of reach of children. In case of accidental overdose, contact Poison Control immediately.
  • Consult your physician before use if you are pregnant, nursing, taking medications, or have any medical condition.
