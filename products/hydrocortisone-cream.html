About This Product

Hydrocortisone 1% Cream is an OTC corticosteroid cream that temporarily relieves itching and minor skin irritations. Its anti-inflammatory action reduces redness, swelling, and itching associated with a wide range of common skin conditions.

What It Treats

  • Eczema and atopic dermatitis
  • Psoriasis (minor flare-ups)
  • Seborrheic dermatitis
  • Insect bites and stings
  • Poison ivy, poison oak, and poison sumac rashes
  • Contact dermatitis from soaps, detergents, cosmetics, or jewelry
  • Minor skin irritations and rashes
  • External anal and genital itching

Directions for Use

Read all product information before use. Keep out of reach of children.
  • Adults and children 2 years of age and older:
    • Apply to affected area not more than 3 to 4 times daily.
    • Apply a thin layer and rub in gently.
  • Children under 2 years of age: Do not use. Ask a doctor.
  • For external anal and genital itching:
    • Adults: Apply to affected area up to 3 to 4 times daily.
    • Children under 12 years: Ask a doctor.
    • When practical, clean the affected area with mild soap and warm water and rinse thoroughly before applying cream.
    • Gently dry by patting or blotting with toilet tissue or a soft cloth before applying cream.

Important Warnings

For external use only. Do not use in or near the eyes. Do not use for the treatment of diaper rash.
  • Do not use if you have a vaginal discharge, as this may be a sign of a condition for which you should see a doctor.
  • Ask a doctor before use if you have:
    • A skin condition that covers a large area of the body
    • Any bleeding from the rectum
  • When using this product:
    • Avoid contact with the eyes.
    • Do not use more than directed unless directed by a doctor.
    • Do not put directly into the rectum using fingers, pipes, or nozzles.
  • Stop use and ask a doctor if:
    • Condition worsens, symptoms persist for more than 7 days, or symptoms clear up and occur again within a few days.
    • Rectal bleeding occurs.
  • If pregnant or breastfeeding, ask a health professional before use.
  • Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact Poison Control immediately.
