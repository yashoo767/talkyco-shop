About This Product

Ibuprofen 200mg Tablets are a trusted over-the-counter pain reliever and fever reducer for adults and children 12 years and older. Each coated tablet contains 200mg of ibuprofen USP.

Ibuprofen is classified as a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID). It works by reducing hormones that cause pain and inflammation in the body.

What It Treats

  • Headache and migraine pain
  • Muscle aches and pains
  • Backache
  • Minor arthritis pain
  • Menstrual cramps
  • Common cold and flu symptoms including fever and body aches
  • Toothache

Directions for Use

Read all product information before use. Keep out of reach of children.
  • Adults and children 12 years and over: Take 1 tablet every 4 to 6 hours while symptoms persist.
  • If pain or fever does not respond to 1 tablet, 2 tablets may be used.
  • Use the smallest effective dose.
  • Do not exceed 3 tablets in 24 hours unless directed by a doctor.
  • Children under 12 years: Ask a doctor.

Take with food or milk if stomach upset occurs. Swallow tablets whole with a full glass of water.

Important Warnings

Allergy alert: Ibuprofen may cause a severe allergic reaction, especially in people allergic to aspirin. Symptoms may include hives, facial swelling, asthma (wheezing), shock, skin reddening, rash, or blisters.
  • Stomach bleeding warning: This product contains an NSAID, which may cause severe stomach bleeding.
  • Do not use if you have ever had an allergic reaction to any other pain reliever or fever reducer.
  • Do not use right before or after heart surgery.
  • Ask a doctor before use if you have had stomach problems, heart disease, high blood pressure, kidney or liver disease.
  • Ask a doctor or pharmacist if you are taking blood thinners (anticoagulants) or any other drug.
  • Stop use and ask a doctor if pain gets worse or lasts more than 10 days, fever gets worse or lasts more than 3 days.
  • If pregnant or breastfeeding, ask a health professional before use. Avoid use at 20 weeks or later in pregnancy.
  • Keep out of reach of children. In case of overdose, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center immediately.
