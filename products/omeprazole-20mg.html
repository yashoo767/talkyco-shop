About This Product

Omeprazole 20mg Delayed-Release Capsules are a proton pump inhibitor (PPI) that works by reducing the amount of acid the stomach produces. Unlike antacids that neutralize acid after it has been produced, omeprazole blocks acid production at the source.

For Frequent Heartburn

For adults who have heartburn 2 or more days per week. Not intended for occasional or immediate heartburn relief.

  • Treats the root cause of frequent heartburn
  • One capsule per day for 14 days
  • May take 1–4 days for full effect
  • Complete the 14-day course even if symptoms improve

Directions

This product is to be used once daily (every 24 hours), every day for 14 days.
  • Adults 18 years and over: Swallow 1 capsule with a glass of water before eating in the morning.
  • Take every day for 14 days.
  • You may repeat a 14-day course every 4 months.
  • Do not take for more than 14 days or more often than every 4 months unless directed by a doctor.
  • Do not crush or chew capsules. May open capsule and sprinkle on 1 tablespoon of applesauce if needed.
  • Children under 18: Ask a doctor before use.

Warnings

Do not use if you have trouble or pain swallowing food, vomiting with blood, or bloody or black stools. These may be signs of a serious condition.
  • Ask a doctor before use if you have had heartburn for over 3 months, frequent chest pain, heartburn with lightheadedness, sweating or dizziness, liver disease, or you take clopidogrel, methotrexate, atazanavir, or nelfinavir.
  • Stop use and ask a doctor if heartburn continues or worsens, if you need to take this product for more than 14 days, or if you need to take this more than once every 4 months.
  • Clostridium difficile-associated diarrhea (CDAD): Long-term use of PPIs may increase risk. Contact your doctor if diarrhea persists.
  • Bone fracture warning: Prolonged use may increase risk of bone fractures.
  • If pregnant or breastfeeding, ask a health professional before use.
  • Keep out of reach of children.
