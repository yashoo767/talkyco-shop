About This Supplement

Vitamin D3 + Calcium Complex combines two essential nutrients for comprehensive bone and immune health support. Vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol) is the natural form of Vitamin D produced by the body when skin is exposed to sunlight. It plays a critical role in helping the body absorb calcium effectively.

Key Benefits

  • Supports strong bones and teeth
  • Promotes healthy calcium absorption
  • Supports immune system function
  • May help maintain muscle function
  • Easy-to-swallow softgel format
