Processing Time

Orders placed before 2:00 PM Eastern Time (ET) on business days (Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays) are typically processed and shipped the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM ET or on weekends and holidays will be processed the next business day.

During high-volume periods (holidays, promotions), processing may take an additional 1–2 business days.

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays. Delivery estimates are not guaranteed and may vary based on carrier conditions, weather, and other factors beyond our control.

Eligible Shipping Locations

TalkyCo Shop ships to eligible addresses within the contiguous United States (48 states). We do not currently ship to:

  • Alaska or Hawaii
  • U.S. territories (Puerto Rico, Guam, U.S. Virgin Islands, etc.)
  • P.O. Boxes or military APO/FPO/DPO addresses
  • International addresses

We hope to expand our shipping coverage in the future.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive an email confirmation with a tracking number and a link to track your shipment. You can also view order status by logging in to your My Account page.

Allow up to 24 hours for tracking information to update after you receive your shipping confirmation email.

Damaged or Lost Shipments

If your order arrives damaged or is lost in transit, please contact us within 7 days of the expected delivery date at support@talkyco.com or 1-800-TALKYCO with your order number and photos of any damage. We will work to resolve the issue promptly with a replacement or refund.

Address Accuracy

Please ensure your shipping address is complete and accurate at checkout. TalkyCo is not responsible for delays or non-deliveries resulting from incorrect, incomplete, or undeliverable addresses provided by the customer. If a package is returned to us due to an incorrect address, we will contact you to arrange re-shipment (additional shipping fees may apply).
