Program Description

The TalkyCo SMS Marketing Program provides recurring marketing text messages about eligible OTC products, promotions, discounts, special offers, new products, and product availability.

The TalkyCo SMS Marketing Program is intended for marketing communications concerning eligible OTC products and related promotional offers. SMS messages do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. TalkyCo does not send prescription information, prescription updates, prescription refills, prescription availability notices, telehealth SMS, or medical reminders via this program.

Consent & Opt-In

By voluntarily checking the SMS marketing consent checkbox and submitting your mobile number, you agree to receive up to 4 recurring marketing text messages per month from TalkyCo regarding eligible OTC products, promotions, discounts, special offers, new products, and product availability.

End users opt in by visiting https://shop.talkyco.com and entering their mobile phone number in the SMS subscription form. Users must actively select an unchecked SMS consent checkbox — it is never pre-selected.

The consent disclosure displayed at opt-in states:

  • That messages are recurring and marketing in nature
  • The message frequency (up to 4 per month)
  • That message and data rates may apply
  • How to reply STOP to unsubscribe
  • How to reply HELP for assistance
  • That consent is not required to make a purchase
  • Links to SMS Terms and Privacy Policy

The SMS consent checkbox is unchecked by default. No marketing SMS messages are sent to users who have not affirmatively opted in.

The opt-in mechanism is publicly verifiable at: https://shop.talkyco.com/sms-consent

Message Frequency

Message frequency: Up to 4 messages per month.

Messages may include:

  • New OTC product announcements
  • Special offers and promotional discounts on eligible OTC products
  • Product restocks and availability updates
  • Promotions, discounts, and special offers on eligible OTC products

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Text message rates and data charges depend on your mobile carrier plan and device. TalkyCo does not charge a fee to participate in the SMS program. Please check with your mobile carrier if you have questions about applicable charges.

How to Stop (Opt Out)

To stop receiving marketing messages, reply STOP to any TalkyCo marketing text message. After you send STOP, you will receive a confirmation message and will no longer receive TalkyCo marketing messages unless you subsequently provide new consent.

You may also unsubscribe from TalkyCo marketing text messages at any time by replying STOP to any TalkyCo marketing text message.

Upon receiving your STOP reply, you will receive one final text message confirming your unsubscription:

"You have been unsubscribed from TalkyCo marketing messages. No further messages will be sent. Visit shop.talkyco.com or call 1-800-TALKYCO for customer support."

After unsubscribing, you will receive no further marketing text messages from TalkyCo. You may re-subscribe at any time by visiting shop.talkyco.com and completing the opt-in form.

You can also opt out by emailing sms@talkyco.com or calling 1-800-TALKYCO.

HELP

For help, reply HELP to any TalkyCo marketing text message or contact TalkyCo customer support at the support contact information provided on this website. You will receive a response with program information and support contact details:

"TalkyCo SMS: Up to 4 marketing msgs/month about eligible OTC products. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. For support: support@talkyco.com or 1-800-TALKYCO. shop.talkyco.com/sms-terms"

You can also get help at:

  • Email: sms@talkyco.com
  • Phone: 1-800-TALKYCO (Monday–Friday, 9 AM–6 PM ET)
  • Contact form

Consent Is Not a Condition of Purchase

Consent to receive marketing text messages is not a condition of purchasing products from TalkyCo. You can make a purchase, create an account, and use all Shop features without providing a phone number or opting into SMS messages.

Supported Carriers

Supported carriers may include all major U.S. mobile carriers including AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Sprint, and other regional carriers. Carrier participation may vary. The program may not be available on all carriers.

Privacy

TalkyCo does not sell or rent mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in information for third-party marketing purposes. Mobile information and SMS consent records will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information may be shared with service providers solely as necessary to provide SMS delivery, customer support, order fulfillment, payment processing, security, and other operational services. Such service providers are not permitted to use mobile information for their own marketing purposes.

For full details on how we handle your information, see our Privacy Policy.

Program Scope

The TalkyCo SMS Marketing Program is intended for marketing communications concerning eligible OTC products and related promotional offers. SMS messages do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. This program does not include prescription drug marketing, prescription updates, telehealth services, or medical reminders.
